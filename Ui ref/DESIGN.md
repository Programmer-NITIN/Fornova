## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural living, bespoke residential craft, and restrained contemporary luxury. Designed for an audience of discerning homeowners, property collectors, and interior connoisseurs, the aesthetic centers on poise, proportion, and quiet confidence.

The design movement is **Warm Minimalism blended with Editorial Modernism**. Rather than sterile tech minimalism or loud maximalism, the visual tone relies on generous negative space, tactile linen surfaces, warm metallic brass accents, and structured typography reminiscent of high-fashion architectural monographs. Every screen should feel like a curated gallery view: serene, grounded, and unmistakably bespoke.

## Colors

The palette directly translates the tactile warmth of natural architectural elements: fine travertine, hand-brushed champagne brass, warm lime-wash linen, and midnight architectural charcoal.

- **Primary (`#B88E4F` - Golden Amber Brass):** Reserved for focal emblems, delicate framing borders, active indicators, and high-prestige CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#1A1918` - Refined Charcoal Noir):** Deep, soft black with a drop of warm umber. Used for high-contrast headlines, primary button fills, and structural framing.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF67` - Champagne Brass):** Used for subtle glowing highlights, fine hairpins, decorative line rules, and hover elevations.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Alabaster / Warm Linen):** The tactile canvas background that replaces harsh pure whites with a serene, natural plaster warmth.

### Functional Roles & Subtones
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F6F0` (Linen Base)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Sheet):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Alabaster)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Tonal Inset):** `#F3EFE7` (Raw Silk Stone)
- **Border / Hairline Accent:** `rgba(184, 142, 79, 0.28)` (Muted Warm Gold)
- **Text Primary:** `#1A1918`
- **Text Muted:** `#736D65`
- **Text Caption / Accent:** `#947844`

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between classic architectural permanence and geometric clarity.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Unapologetically editorial, poetic, and refined. Employed for master hero headlines, architectural suite titles, and quote blocks. Keeps text airy, breathing, and elevated.
- **Body & Structural Elements (`DM Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and effortless to read. It offsets the flourish of the serif with modern architectural rigor.
- **All-Caps Editorial Rules:** Labels, category markers, and navigation triggers strictly use `label-caps` with heavy letter-spacing (`0.2em` to `0.25em`) and uppercase transformation to evoke luxury brand crests and floor-plan annotations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an architectural gallery portfolio: fixed max-width frames (`1400px`) centered on an expansive alabaster field with generous vertical cadence.

- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous margins are mandatory. Content must never touch canvas bounds; generous space breathes intentionality and exclusivity.
- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (80px), `gutter-desktop` (40px). Asymmetrical spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text opposed to 7-column architectural photography) reinforce high-fashion composition.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, 32px margins, 24px gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid grid, 24px margins, 16px gutters with full-bleed imagery capabilities.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects heavy, synthetic drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast brass hairpins, warm ambient under-glows, and tactile surface layers**.

1. **Flat / Resting Tier:** Pure Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`) against Canvas Base (`#F9F6F0`), demarcated solely by an ultra-fine 1px border (`rgba(184, 142, 79, 0.22)`).
2. **Elevated / Hover Tier:** Ambient shadow tinted with warm umber and brass: `0 14px 34px -10px rgba(45, 36, 24, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(184, 142, 79, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating / Modal Tier:** Backdrop filter with frosted warm glass (`rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a golden hairline.
4. **Dividers:** Fine horizontal rules with centered diamond glyphs (`◆`) or subtle brass gradients that fade from transparent to `#B88E4F` to transparent.

## Shapes

The shape geometry is **Sharp (`0`)**. 

Architecture is defined by crisp lines, precise miters, and structured planes. Curved or bubbly corners soften structural authority; therefore, cards, interactive buttons, modal frames, and image containers maintain disciplined 0px right angles. 

The single exception is circular iconography badges (such as circular contact medallions), which represent complete geometrical circles (`border-radius: 9999px`) creating purposeful architectural contrast against orthogonal planes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Charcoal (`#1A1918`) fill, sharp corners (`0px`), text in Alabaster (`#F9F6F0`), uppercase `label-caps` font. Hover state transitions background smoothly into Golden Amber (`#B88E4F`).
- **Secondary / Outlined Button:** Transparent fill, 1px perimeter border in `#B88E4F`, text in `#1A1918` with letter-spacing `0.2em`. Hover state introduces a faint champagne wash (`rgba(184, 142, 79, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link CTA:** Inline link accompanied by an elongated thin hairline arrow (`→`), underlined by a delicate 1px rule placed 4px below text baseline.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Architectural Project Card:** Rectangular image block with 0px radius, wrapped in a 1px border of `rgba(184, 142, 79, 0.2)`. Hover triggers a micro-zoom (scale 1.02) on the photograph while an overlay reveals project title in `headline-sm` serif.
- **Specification Sheet Card:** Flat `#FFFFFF` interior surface, subtle top border highlight in champagne brass, with metadata arranged in alternating architectural rows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Strict uppercase (`label-caps`), bordered in `#B88E4F` with an off-white fill (`#F9F6F0`). Internal padding: `4px 14px`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist architectural underline or hairline box (`#D4AF67` at 40% opacity). Label floats above in `label-caps` muted charcoal. On focus, the border turns to solid `#B88E4F` with no glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Form Controls:** Checkboxes are sharp square boxes (0px border-radius) with an inner brass fill upon selection. Radio buttons are concentric brass rings.

### Decorative Separators
- **Brand Line Dividers:** Thin horizontal lines (`1px`) styled with a centered diamond badge ornament, echoing high-end architectural catalog flourishes.
