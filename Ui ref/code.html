FAQ / Inquiry Guide

Frequently Asked.

Everything you need to know before initiating an architectural commission with our atelier.

01. Do you handle landmark permits and NYC DOB approvals? add

Yes. Our atelier handles 100% of municipal filings, NYC Landmarks Preservation Commission (LPC) approvals, and DOB architectural filings with licensed in-house expedition.

02. Can I experience 3D models and VR before construction? add

Absolutely. Before ordering materials or opening walls, we deliver photorealistic 3D renders with exact daylight trajectories and full VR headset walkthroughs of your future residence.

03. Can you work within our established budget? add

Yes. We believe luxury architectural craft thrives on discipline. We create transparent line-item milestone budgets and fixed-fee agreements with zero concealed contractor markups.

04. Do you work directly with our preferred general contractor? add

We can either provide turnkey general contracting through our vetted guild of master craftsmen or integrate seamlessly as design architects managing site quality audits.

05. Can I make alterations during the design phases? add

Yes. Our iterative schematic and conceptual phases include up to three comprehensive design rounds with material sample boards before final shop drawings are frozen.

06. How are project fees structured? add

We utilize transparent milestone-tied architectural design retainers accompanied by fixed percentages for procurement, offering clients trade pricing discounts.

07. Do you manage custom furniture and antique procurement? add

Yes. We design custom 1-of-1 millwork pieces, attend European auction viewings, and coordinate international freight, crating, and white-glove unboxing.

08. What is the typical timeline for a luxury residence? add

Full penthouse or estate commissions typically span 8 to 16 months from initial spatial schematic drafting through turnkey completion and staging.
