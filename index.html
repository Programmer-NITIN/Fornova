We eliminate middleman markups through in-house factory fabrication, ensuring transparent pricing and generational durability.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about commissioning Fornova Interior for your Vadodara residence.

What is included in Fornova's Turnkey Interior service?
add

Our turnkey service is 100% end-to-end. We manage 2D space planning, photorealistic 3D visualization, civil alterations, electrical rewiring, false ceiling cove lighting, Italian marble/tile laying, modular kitchen fabrication, wardrobe carpentry, custom beds & sofas, painting, wallpaper, and final deep cleaning. You receive a fully move-in ready home.

How do you manage projects for NRI clients living in the USA, UK, or Canada?
add

Over 40% of our luxury commissions come from Gujarati families residing abroad. We provide a complete remote execution desk: design consultations via Zoom, digital material approval kits, weekly high-definition site video walk-throughs over WhatsApp, and strictly staged milestone payments based on verified physical progress.

How long does a full home interior project typically take in Vadodara?
add

A standard 3 BHK residential interior is executed within 45 to 60 working days from the date of 3D design approval. Sprawling 4 BHK penthouses and independent bungalows typically require 70 to 90 days. We provide a guaranteed completion date in our written contract.

Is Vastu Shastra integrated into your modern designs?
add

Absolutely. We believe modern aesthetics and ancient architectural sciences should coexist in harmony. We ensure the Mandir is positioned in the North-East (Ishanya), the culinary hob in the South-East (Agni), and master bed orientations respect magnetic cardinal directions without compromising clean minimalist geometry.

What materials and hardware brands do you use?
add

We strictly utilize calibrated, BWP/BWR marine grade plywood (IS:710 certified) treated against termites and borers. For hardware and channels, we partner exclusively with world-renowned German engineering brands including Häfele, Hettich, and Blum, backed by manufacturer guarantees.
